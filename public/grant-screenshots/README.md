## 🎨 Technical & Design Specifications

- **Device Dimensions:** 390 × 844 pt viewport (iPhone 14 / modern Android benchmark standard).
- **Export Resolution:** 2x Retina DPI (`780 × 1688 px`), 24-bit sRGB PNG with alpha transparency frame shadow.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Primary Brand Green: `#15803d` (Green 700)
  - Light Emerald Tint: `#dcfce7` / `#ecfdf5`
  - High Contrast Dark Slate: `#0f172a`
  - Background: Solid Clean White (`#ffffff`)
- **Typography & Ergonomics:**
  - Standard system font stack (`-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `Roboto`)
  - 44px+ minimum touch targets for all primary buttons and interactive tabs
  - High accessible contrast meeting WCAG 2.1 AA standards
- **Format Availability:** Both high-res PNG (ready for insertion into Word, PowerPoint, PDF grant forms) and vector SVG (lossless scaling for pitch presentations).
